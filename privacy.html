BeeMi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: BeeMi does not collect any data. Your information stays on your iPhone (and in your private iCloud if you turn it on). There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Who is responsible?

BeeMi is published by Sébastien Billon as an individual. Contact: AppsMi.app@gmail.com.

Data you enter

Apiaries, hives, queens, histories, photos, honey batches, sales, notes and beekeeper profile are stored only on your device. The publisher has no access to them and never receives them.

iCloud

If iCloud is turned on for your device, BeeMi can sync your data between your devices through your private iCloud space. This data is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; the publisher has no access to it.

Backups and sharing

Backups, PDFs (sheets, reports, declarations) and QR codes are created on your device. You decide whether to save, send or print them. You are responsible for keeping your backups in a safe place.

Weather service

To show an apiary's weather, BeeMi queries the Open-Meteo service (open-meteo.com) and sends only the geographic coordinates of the apiary. BeeMi transmits no information that identifies you, but, as with any website, the service can see the IP address of your connection.

Permissions requested

  • Camera and photo library: take or choose pictures of your apiaries and hives, and scan QR codes. Photos stay inside the app.
  • Location (while using the app): place an apiary and show its weather. It is not kept outside your own data.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: dictate notes. Transcription is performed by Apple; depending on your device, audio may be processed on the device or sent to Apple (see Apple's policies).
  • Notifications: reminders and alerts that you schedule. They are created locally.

You can refuse or withdraw each permission in the iPhone Settings; only the related features are then unavailable.

BeeMi Pro purchase

The in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple (App Store). The publisher receives neither your payment details nor your identity.

Your customers' data and the user's responsibility

BeeMi lets you keep customer records (name, contact details, purchases, gifts). This information is personal data: you alone are responsible for this file under data-protection law (GDPR in the European Union), not the publisher of BeeMi, who never has access to it and never receives it.

Depending on your situation, it is up to you in particular to:

  • check whether data-protection law applies to your use: gifts to relatives fall under personal activity, while selling honey professionally is an activity to which it applies;
  • keep a record of your processing activities where required (in France, no prior declaration to the CNIL has been required since 2018);
  • inform your customers how their data is used, collect only what is useful, and avoid sensitive data;
  • answer their requests for access, correction or erasure, and set a retention period;
  • protect your device (passcode or Face ID) and your backups.

The publisher cannot be held responsible for compliance with these obligations. To help you, BeeMi can generate a record template pre-filled from your profile (Settings → Data and privacy → GDPR record for my customers), and the CNIL website (cnil.fr) also offers templates and advice. This is provided for information and is not legal advice.

No tracking, no advertising

BeeMi uses no analytics tools, no advertising and no third-party tracking services, and shares no data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept as long as you keep the app. You can erase it at any time from Settings → “Erase all my data”, or by deleting the app. iCloud data can be deleted in the iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

BeeMi is not directed at children and collects no data about them.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, the publisher cannot be held liable for data loss (lost or broken device, uninstallation, handling error), input or calculation errors, or the consequences of any decision made from the app. BeeMi does not replace a beekeeper's regulatory obligations.

Changes

This policy may change; the update date is shown at the top of the page.
